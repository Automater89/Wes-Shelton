        Experience

        I started in health-plan member support, then spent years in benefits: enrollment and life-event changes, COBRA and dependent eligibility, EDI 834 eligibility files, vendor data, audits, and open-enrollment presentations to groups of 300 or more. The systems were PeopleSoft, Workday, and ADP Workforce Now: I managed benefits data in PeopleSoft, corrected enrollment records and tested benefits releases with written checklists in Workday, and set up annual enrollment and configured deduction codes and eligibility rules in ADP Workforce Now. In two earlier roles I also sent eligibility files to carriers and monitored the scheduled transfers.

        In my most recent benefits role I ran annual open enrollment for the health and welfare plans from start to finish (project plan, communications, ADP Workforce Now configuration and testing, and vendor coordination) and managed benefits projects such as the ADP Mobile benefits rollout. I prepared Form 5500 filings, ACA 1095-C reporting and responses to IRS and DOL audits for final approval by my manager and director, processed 401(k) deferral changes, contributions, loans and hardship withdrawals through a recordkeeper change, and developed a 401(k) and medical-plan RFP analysis covering about 2,000 North American employees.

        In December 2024 I moved into IT as an IT Project Manager / AI Enablement Specialist. I managed the ServiceNow Application Portfolio Management lifecycle for 100+ applications, from business demand intake through delivery and retirement, and worked in ServiceNow daily across incidents, requests, demand and reports. For each new software request I reviewed single sign-on, API and data-feed connections with IT security and recorded license counts, renewals, costs and vendor quotes. I also built and piloted automation, developed 500+ governed prompts with responsible-AI documentation, and supported people learning new tools. I hold the ServiceNow IT Leadership Professional Certificate.

        The systems work started earlier, in benefits: I managed the project that replaced a paper tuition-reimbursement form with a SharePoint approval workflow, working with a colleague who brought the SharePoint and Power Automate expertise. Those years are why I ask how a change will land for the employee on the other end.

        How I Work

        1. Start with the process.

          Understand the work and responsibilities before choosing a tool. On HR offboarding, I gathered HR and IT requirements and wrote the charter and test plan before building anything.

        2. Choose a practical solution.

          Use AI where it fits and a straightforward workflow where it does not.

        3. Make the handoff clear.

          Document the process, testing, user guidance, and responsibilities.

        4. Separate plans from results.

          State what was built, what was tested, and what remains a proposal. That is why the SCORM project here is labeled as a pilot.
