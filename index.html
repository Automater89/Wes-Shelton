        Experience

The systems were PeopleSoft, Workday, and ADP Workforce Now: I managed benefits data in PeopleSoft, corrected enrollment records and tested benefits releases with written checklists in Workday, and set up annual enrollment and configured deduction codes and eligibility rules in ADP Workforce Now.

The systems work started earlier, in benefits: I managed the project that replaced a paper tuition-reimbursement form with a SharePoint approval workflow, working with a colleague who brought the SharePoint and Power Automate expertise. Those years are why I ask how a change will land for the employee on the other end.

        How I Work

        1. Start with the process.

          Understand the work and responsibilities before choosing a tool. On HR offboarding, I gathered HR and IT requirements and wrote the charter and test plan before building anything.

        2. Choose a practical solution.

          Use AI where it fits and a straightforward workflow where it does not.

        3. Make the handoff clear.

          Document the process, testing, user guidance, and responsibilities.

        4. Separate plans from results.

          State what was built, what was tested, and what remains a proposal. That is why the SCORM project here is labeled as a pilot.
